# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
ability, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback focused on code and outcomes.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Accept correction and prioritize project quality over personal preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks,
deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive behavior are
not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers or through GitHub's
private reporting features. Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.
